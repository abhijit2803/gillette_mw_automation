# ADP Regression Checks

## **Objective**
Conduct a regression checks of Articles in the GERMANY website

**Regression Steps**

# Step 1: URL Navigation 
**Test Step**: Navigate to https://www.gillette.de/de-de/perfekte-rasur and wait until the page fully loads.
**Expected Result**: The Article Listing Page displays successfully, showing all articles with valid titles, and corresponding ADP URLs.

# Step 2: Cookie Consent
**Test Step**: Once cookies consent is loaded, click on 'Accept Cookie Consent' button
**Expected Result**: 'Accept Cookie Consent' button is successfully clicked and the pop-up disppears.

# Step 3: Search for Article in ALP
**Test Step**: Search for 'Anleitung für die richtige Rasur der Bartkonturen' article using visible text or accessible roles.
**Expected Result**: The article from the list is located, and its title along with the corresponding ADP URL is identified.

# Step 4: Selection of Article in ALP
**Test Step**: Select the 'Anleitung für die richtige Rasur der Bartkonturen' article card and click on its title
**Expected Result**: The Article Details Page opens, displaying the same article title as shown on the Article Listing Page.

# Step 5: Verify Facebook share functionality
**Test Step**: Click on the Facebook icon using visible text or role.
**Expected Result**: The Facebook icon is clicked successfully, and the corresponding pop-up window appears.

# Step 6: Verify Facebook share functionality
**Test Step**: Close the Facebook pop-up using the close icon.
**Expected Result**: The pop-up closes successfully and is removed from the screen.

# Step 7: Verify Copy functionality
**Test Step**: Click on the Copy icon using visible text or role.
**Expected Result**: The Copy icon is clicked successfully, and the corresponding pop-up window appears.

# Step 8: Verify Copy functionality
**Test Step**: Close the Copy pop-up using the close icon.
**Expected Result**: The pop-up closes successfully and is removed from the screen.

# Step 9: Verify Favourite (Bookmark) functionality
**Test Step**: Click on the Favorite icon using visible text or role.
**Expected Result**: The Favorite icon is clicked successfully, and the icon is filled with blue colour and icon is bold.

# Step 10: Verify Article Recommender
**Test Step**: Click on the Thumbs Up icon using visible text or role and wait for 1 minute.
**Expected Result**: The Thumbs Up icon is clicked successfully, and the icon is filled with blue colour and icon is bold. Also, Thumbs down button disappears.

# Step 11: Verify Related Product Section
**Test Step**: Click and Verify the Related Products section
**Expected Result**: Each product card shows image, title, and link. And once clicked on the card, linked URL navigates to correct PDP.

# Step 12: Verify BUY NOW function on Related Product Section
**Test Step**: Click and Verify the BUY NOW function on Related Products section
**Expected Result**: Once click on the BUY NOW button, BUY NOW popup appears.

# Step 13: Verify Related Article Section
**Test Step**: Click and Verify the Related Article section
**Expected Result**: Each article card shows image, title, and link.

# Step 14: Verify READ MORE functionality of Related Article Section
**Test Step**: Click on the READ MORE button.
**Expected Result**: The READ MORE button is clicked successfully, and the linked URL navigates to correct ADP.

# Step 15: Verify the User Interface of the page
**Test Step**: Verify the font family, letter spacing, font size, margins, image sizes  are aligned.
**Expected Result**: All the UI structure of the page is intact with all sections visible and no break in the page is present.
